Q271. Article 112 of Indian Constitution deals with?
A) Fundamental Rights✅ B) Annual Financial Statement (Union Budget)
C) EmergencyD) Amendments
💡 Budget = Annual Financial Statement; presented by FM in Parliament

A) Budget✅ B) Special status of J&K (abrogated Aug 5, 2019)
C) EmergencyD) GST
💡 Revoked; J&K split into 2 UTs: J&K + Ladakh
Q273. Article 21 guarantees?
A) Equality✅ B) Right to Life & Personal Liberty
C) Freedom of speechD) Education
💡 Most important Fundamental Right; includes right to privacy (SC 2017)
Q274. Article 32 is called?
A) Right to Life✅ B) Right to Constitutional Remedies (Heart & Soul of Constitution)
C) Right to EqualityD) Right to Education
💡 Dr Ambedkar called it "Heart & Soul"; 5 writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Certiorari, Prohibition, Quo Warranto
Q275. Article 279A deals with?
A) Budget✅ B) GST Council
C) EmergencyD) Finance Commission
💡 101st Amendment; GST Council headed by FM; 37 members
Q276. Article 21A (86th Amendment) provides?
A) Right to vote✅ B) Right to Education (age 6-14)
C) Right to healthD) Right to food
💡 RTE Act 2009; free & compulsory education; Fundamental Right
Q277. 73rd Amendment (1992) deals with?
A) GST✅ B) Panchayati Raj (local self-government)
C) EmergencyD) Budget
💡 Part IX; 3-tier: Gram Panchayat, Block, District; 1/3 women reservation
Q278. 74th Amendment (1992) deals with?
A) Panchayat✅ B) Municipalities (Urban local bodies)
C) GSTD) Courts
💡 Part IXA; Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation
Q279. 101st Amendment (2016) introduced?
A) Panchayat✅ B) GST (Goods & Services Tax)
C) DemonetisationD) RTE
💡 "One Nation One Tax"; effective Jul 1, 2017; replaced 17 indirect taxes
